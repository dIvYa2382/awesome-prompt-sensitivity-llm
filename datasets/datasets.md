# Datasets and Benchmarks

## 1. MMLU — Massive Multitask Language Understanding

**Source:** Hendrycks et al.

**Purpose:** MMLU evaluates language models across a wide range of academic and professional subjects.

**Application to this topic:** MMLU can be used to test whether changing prompt wording or structure changes model performance across different knowledge domains.

**Link:** https://github.com/hendrycks/test

---

## 2. GSM8K

**Source:** OpenAI

**Purpose:** GSM8K is a dataset of grade-school mathematical word problems designed for evaluating mathematical reasoning.

**Application to this topic:** Multiple prompt variants can be applied to the same questions to measure whether the generated reasoning and final answers remain stable.

**Link:** https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math

---

## 3. BIG-bench

**Source:** Google Research and collaborators

**Purpose:** BIG-bench is a large collection of tasks designed to evaluate the capabilities and limitations of language models.

**Application to this topic:** It can be used to study prompt sensitivity across different types of tasks instead of relying on a single benchmark.

**Link:** https://github.com/google/BIG-bench
